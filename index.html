Round 08

Questions, answered.

How the scoring works, what the safety gates refuse to allow, and what it costs.

AlgorithmSafetyCompliancePrivacyPricingLaunch

Every pairing runs through six weighted gates: age band, weight gap, experience spread, governing-body medical rest, accumulated head-contact load and licence classification. Each gate returns its own verdict and a 0–100 contribution. The lowest-scoring gate drives the overall verdict, so a single hard failure can never be averaged away by strong scores elsewhere.
